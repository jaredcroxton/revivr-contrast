Welcome to Revivr

A wellness camp, not a gym membership.

The Revivr Club Wellness Camp and Day Experience takes you on a powerful journey through hand-selected wellness partner locations where nature, movement and community come together. From cold water dips and grounding breathwork to social connection and mindfulness-based workshops, every activity is designed to boost your mental clarity and create sustainable, positive habit change.

The Protocol

Four things, in the right order, with someone watching.

01

Cold

Cold water immersion at around eleven degrees. Two to three minutes, coached the whole way, scaled to the person standing in the tub. It is controlled exposure, never a dare.

Cold water dips
02

Breath

Grounding breathwork before you get in and after you get out. This is the part that turns a cold dip into a nervous system reset instead of a shock.

Grounding breathwork
03

Heat

Sauna. Ideal for loosening up muscles, and the half of contrast therapy everyone actually looks forward to. Rounds run hot and long enough to matter.

Sauna · contrast rounds
04

Stillness

Mindfulness-based workshops, yoga and pilates. Great for mind and body wellbeing, and the reason people come back after the novelty of the ice wears off.

Yoga · pilates · workshops

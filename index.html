Welcome to Revivr

A wellness camp, not a gym membership.

The Revivr Club Wellness Camp and Day Experience takes you on a powerful journey through hand-selected wellness partner locations where nature, movement and community come together. From cold water dips and grounding breathwork to social connection and mindfulness-based workshops, every activity is designed to boost your mental clarity and create sustainable, positive habit change.

The Protocol

Four things, in the right order, with someone watching.

01

Cold

Cold water immersion at around eleven degrees. Two to three minutes, coached the whole way, scaled to the person standing in the tub. It is controlled exposure, never a dare.

Cold water dips

Grounding breathwork

Before you come

The things everyone asks first.

I have never done cold water before. Can I still come?
Yes, and most people at their first camp have not. The immersion is coached the whole way and scaled to the person standing in the tub, and you go in with a partner rather than alone. It is controlled exposure, never a dare. All levels, all ages.
What do I need to bring?
A towel, and something you can get in the water in. Everything else is set up for you when you arrive.
How long does a camp run?
Around three hours. Arrival and coffee from 06:15, the breath circle at 06:40, the plunge at 07:00, then sauna, movement, contrast rounds, and everyone sitting down together from about 09:10. The full timeline is in A morning at camp.
What actually happens, in order?
Four things, in the right order, with someone watching: cold water immersion, grounding breathwork, sauna, then stillness through yoga, pilates and mindfulness-based workshops. That sequence is the whole method. It is laid out in The Protocol.
Where do the camps run?
Three locations across South East Queensland: the Sunshine Coast home camp at 13 Fairfax Street, Sippy Downs, plus hand-selected partner sites in Brisbane and on the Gold Coast. Each has its own live booking calendar under Book a camp.
Is a camp the same as the coaching packages?
No. A camp is the guided day experience you book through your location calendar. The coaching packages are ongoing personal training and programming you buy separately under Coaching packages.
